# The Rank Tax — Executive Summary
**Bottom line:** Position alone drives most of the top-of-page advantage. Under random order the top 5 slots take 54% of bookings; ~61% of the ranked top-5 advantage is position, not quality.
**Where to act:** 68 high-quality properties are persistently buried (~26 percentile points). Promoting them by 10 slots yields ~+2.7% net revenue (naive estimate ~2x higher, ignores displacement).
**Segment insight:** Rank matters 2.6x more for 90+ day bookers than same-day bookers (premium 5.6x vs 2.2x).
**Next step:** 1–2% traffic interleaving test, KPI = net bookings per search.
**Caveat:** Numbers from synthetic data with the Expedia schema; rerun on the Kaggle file before quoting.
